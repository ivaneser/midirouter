▶ ❓ How to Play

🎹 Clips (pads)

  • Empty clip — press to record; press again to end the take. Recording on a track stops that track's playing clip.
  • Stopped clip — press to play it (Play mode), or to record a new layer on top (Overdub mode).
  • Playing clip — Play mode: press to stop it. Overdub mode: press to record a new layer; press again to end the layer and keep looping.
  • Long-press (≈600 ms) a recorded clip — clears it.
  • Pads act on press only; releasing a pad never stops a clip or a take.

🧱 Tracks & Clips

  • + under a track — adds an empty clip to that track.
  • - under a track — removes the track's last clip. On its only clip, removes the whole track. The last remaining track is kept.
  • + after the last track — adds a track. Limits: 16 tracks, 16 clips per track.

🎛️ Transport

  • ▶ Play — starts the transport: MIDI clock, metronome and playing clips. Press again to stop.
  • ⏹ Stop — stops all playback.
  • Post-record — what a take does when it stops. Play: loops. Overdub: loops and each press records a new layer.
  • Metro — toggles the click; the dropdown sets beats per bar. Tap — tap tempo.
  • Reset — clears all clips, clears track channel assignments, restores the default tracks and clips per track, and sets Post-record to Play.

💾 Sessions

  • 💾 Save — saves all clips, notes, BPM and mode to sessions/<name>.json.
  • ⏬ Load — lists saved sessions to load or delete. The header shows the last 3 sessions for quick load; the loaded one is green.
  • Routing presets (⚙️ MIDI Routing → 💾 Save / 📂 Load) store routing only and are separate from sessions.

🔌 MIDI Routing

  • All connected MIDI devices are routed to each other automatically; hot-plug is supported.
  • Custom routes: ⚙️ MIDI Routing → + Add Route. 🔊 MIDI Devices lists every connected port.
