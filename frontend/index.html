▶ ❓ How to Play

🎹 Using the Pads

  • Empty pad press = record. Press any empty clip to start recording; your notes go into that clip. No arming step needed.
  • Press the same pad again = stop the take — then the Mode decides what happens next (see below).
  • Playing pad — press stops it; press again starts it (Play mode).
  • Press a new empty clip on the same track — stops the current clip and records into the new one.
  • Pads toggle on press; releasing a pad never stops a clip or a take.

🎛️ Transport & Buttons

  • ▶ Play — starts the transport (MIDI clock + metronome + active clips). Press again to stop it (toggle).
  • ⏹ Stop — stops all playback.
  • ● Reset — resets ALL clips to zero (wipe everything), clears track MIDI channel assignments, and sets Mode back to Play for a fresh session.
  • Metro — toggle metronome click. Choose beats with the dropdown. ⏱ MTC — toggle MIDI Clock output for external gear.
  • Tap — tap tempo for BPM.
  • Launchkey buttons: Play (CC115, toggle), Stop (CC116), Reset (CC117, full reset), Loop (CC118).

📝 Post-record (what happens when the initial recording stops)

  • Play — the take starts looping right away. Next press stops the clip; the press after that plays it again.
  • Overdub — the clip keeps looping and records a new layer on top; the next press switches it to Play.
  • Empty clips always record on first press, whatever the Post-record setting.

💾 Sessions (save/load recordings)

  • 💾 Save (header, left of the connection status) — ask for a name, saves the whole recording (all clips, notes, BPM, mode) to sessions/<name>.json.
  • ⏬ Load (header) — opens a list of saved sessions; click one to load it into the DAW.
  • Header quick-load: the last 3 saved sessions are shown left of 💾 Save; the currently loaded project is green.
  • Separate from router presets (⚙️ MIDI Routing → Save/Load), which store routing/port configuration.

🔌 MIDI Routing

  • All connected MIDI devices are auto-linked (all-to-all routing); hot-plug works.
  • 🔊 MIDI Devices shows every connected port. Add custom routes in ⚙️ MIDI Routing to route specific inputs to outputs.

💡 Pad LEDs (Launchkey Mini MK3)

  • Flashing cyan — the last activated clip, playing.
  • Steady cyan — another clip that is playing.
  • Pulsing cyan — clip has a recording but is stopped.
  • Flashing red — recording in progress. Off — empty clip.
