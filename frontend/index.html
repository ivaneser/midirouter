▶ ❓ How to Play

🎹 Using LaunchKey Pads

  • Click a pad once — it auto-connects to the next free track/slot.
  • Press & hold a pad in Replace/Overdub mode — records your notes, releases to play them back.
  • Tap a pad again while a clip plays — stops it.
  • Tap tempo with any pad or use the Tap button to set BPM.

🎛️ Transport Controls

  • ▶ Play — starts playback of the current clip.
  • ⏹ Stop — stops all playback.
  • ● Rec Arm — resets all clips to zero and arms recording (replace mode) for a fresh take.
  • Metro — toggle metronome click. Choose beats with the dropdown.

📝 Mode (what happens after a take ends)

  • An empty clip always starts recording on pad press (any Mode).
  • Pressing a clip that is playing stops it; pressing a stopped clip in Play mode starts it again.
  • Play — when you stop recording, the clip starts looping the take.
  • Overdub — when you stop, recording keeps going: layer new notes on top of the take.
  • Replace — when you stop, the clip stays stopped; the next press on it records a fresh take that overwrites the old one.
  • Pressing a new empty clip stops the current clip on that track and records into the new one.

💾 Saving & Loading

  • Save — store your current DAW state (BPM, modes, clips) to a preset file.
  • Load — restore a previously saved preset.

🔌 MIDI Routing

  • All connected MIDI devices are auto-linked (all-to-all routing).
  • Add custom routes in the Configuration section to route specific inputs to outputs.
  • Devices appear automatically when plugged in (hot-plug supported).
